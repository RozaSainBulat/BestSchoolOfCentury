Privacy Policy of the “Best School of the Century”

Updated: November 17, 2026

Who we are

“Best School of the Century” is a free school in the Telegram bot @best_school_of_century_bot and this website. The author and owner of the project is Roza Truksane.

What data we receive

In the bot: your Telegram chat ID, the name from your Telegram profile, language, grade, lesson progress (which lessons have been completed), review data, submitted homework (text or a photo of a notebook), the sponsor form if you fill it out, and, when you support us with Telegram Stars, the amount and the language you are donating to. We do not receive bank card details: Telegram processes the payment. On the website, we do not collect personal data and do not use cookies; your browser only remembers the language you chose on your device. The website's hosting (GitHub Pages) may keep technical access logs.

Why

To run lessons, check assignments, remind you to review, keep track of support and show the overall counter. Your name is shown on the wall of thanks only with your consent; otherwise “Anonymous”.

What we do not do

We do not sell data, do not use it for advertising and do not share it with third parties, except Telegram as the bot's platform and cases required by law. Only the school's author sees the homework.

Children

Lessons are read to the child by a parent or another adult. We do not ask children for any personal data beyond what is needed for the lesson.

Storage

Data is stored on the author's server while you use the school and is deleted at your request.

Your rights

You can ask us to show, correct or delete your data: write “delete my data” in the bot (the “✏️ Found a mistake?” button or the /paysupport command). We will delete the data within 30 days.

Contact

Via the bot @best_school_of_century_bot.
