How our school differs from others

  1. A proven program, not an invented one. We teach from mid-20th-century school books, using the system under which an entire country became literate within a single generation: in 1926 roughly half of the people could read, and by 1959 almost everyone could. The course is based on Voskresenskaya's primer (1952) and its teacher's methodological guide (1948), and Pchelko's "Arithmetic" for Grade 1 (1959) and its guide. We do not change the content or the order: the student's record shows which books they studied from.
  2. The child reads from the very first pages. Syllables immediately form words: "ma-ma", "ra-ma". The child does not spend months on sounds alone; they see results from the first weeks.
  3. Everyone starts with the first letter. We do not give a placement test and do not let anyone skip topics, so no gaps are left in knowledge. Those who already know the material simply go faster.
  4. Suitable for families where the adults cannot read. soon A voice guide and clear icons: it explains how the lesson goes and what to press. Being able to press buttons is enough.
  5. Learning in the child's native language. Each language is built using our method on its own alphabet and its own words; it is not a translation with subtitles.
  6. The parent does not need to be a teacher. Every lesson comes with: what to do, how many minutes to study, what the child should learn, and what to write with (on a printout, in a notebook, or with a stylus).
  7. Grounded in the science of memory. Reviews follow the forgetting curve: the next day, after 3 days, after a week, after a month. The bot reminds the parent on its own. Sources are attached to the lessons: Ebbinghaus (1885), Murre & Dros (2015), Cepeda et al. (2006), Roediger & Karpicke (2006).
  8. For those starting from scratch. The course is designed for children who have not attended kindergarten or school.
  9. The first grade is free. This includes lessons, a game, and materials for parents.

How the school bot works

The bot in Telegram: t.me/best_school_of_century_bot

  • 19 languages. The bot automatically opens in your phone's language, and you can change the language at any time with the 🌐 button.
  • The whole family in one account. A parent can add several children, each with their own name, their own progress, and their own achievements.
  • Several languages at once. One child can learn two, three, or more languages in parallel, with progress tracked separately for each. For example, the native language at home and the language of school.
  • Adults learn too. You can learn at your own pace and go back. If the language is new, a "Mini Dictionary" soon with words from the lessons is turned on. This is convenient for families who have moved to another country and are learning its language together with their child.
  • A school day, just like in a real school. Every day starts with a schedule. The parent sees in advance what is coming tomorrow, what to prepare, and which reviews are due.
  • Homework is sent directly in the bot. soon The next lesson opens once the previous assignment has been accepted. That way nothing is skipped.
  • Completed material is always at hand: the ✅ Completed and 🔁 Review buttons.
  • Tests at the end of each quarter and a final test at the end of the year. soon They show that the material has truly been learned.
  • A tip for parents. If you know English or French, you can go through the instructions in that language and then explain the lessons to your child in your native language.
  • Lessons will also be available on the website. soon This is for those whose Telegram does not open or is blocked.
