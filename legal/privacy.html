Privacy Policy — Resource Trading No.1

Last updated: 10 November 2026

Who we are

“Resource Trading No.1” is a business Page on Facebook operated by its owner, Roza Truksane, for business introductions in commodities trading. The app “Resource Trading Bot” is a private tool used only by the Page owner to publish posts on that Page and to receive messages sent to that Page.

What data we process

When you send a message to the Page, we receive the content of your message and your Page-scoped Facebook ID. When you comment on a post, we receive the comment. We do not collect data from your personal profile, friends or other pages.

How we use it

Only to read and answer your inquiry and to keep a business record of the conversation. We do not sell your data, do not use it for advertising profiling and do not share it with third parties, except where required by law.

Storage and retention

Messages are stored in the owner’s private business records and kept for as long as needed for the business relationship, then deleted.

Your rights and data deletion

You may ask us to access, correct or delete your data at any time by sending a message to the “Resource Trading No.1” Page with the words “delete my data”. We will delete it within 30 days. You may also remove the app’s access in your Facebook settings under “Business Integrations”.

Contact

Send a message to the Facebook Page “Resource Trading No.1”.
