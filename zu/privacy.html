Inqubomgomo yobumfihlo yesikole «Isikole Esihamba Phambili Sekhulu Leminyaka»

Kubuyekezwe: 17 Novemba 2026

Singobani

«Isikole Esihamba Phambili Sekhulu Leminyaka» — isikole samahhala ku-bot ye-Telegram @best_school_of_century_bot kanye nale webhusayithi. Umbhali nomnikazi wephrojekthi — Roza Truksane.

Imininingwane esiyitholayo

Ku-bot: inombolo ehlonza ingxoxo ye-Telegram, igama elivela kuphrofayili yakho ye-Telegram, ulimi, ibanga, inqubekela phambili yezifundo (yiziphi izifundo eziqediwe), imininingwane yokuphinda, imisebenzi yasekhaya ethunyelwe (umbhalo noma isithombe sencwadi yokubhalela), ifomu lomxhasi uma uligcwalisa, futhi uma usekela ngezinkanyezi ze-Telegram — inani nolimi onikelela lona. Asiyitholi imininingwane yekhadi lasebhange: inkokhelo yenziwa yi-Telegram. Kuwebhusayithi asiqoqi imininingwane yomuntu siqu futhi asisebenzisi ama-cookies; isiphequluli sakho sikhumbula kuphela ulimi olukhethile kudivayisi yakho. Indawo ebamba iwebhusayithi (GitHub Pages) ingagcina amarekhodi obuchwepheshe okuvakashelwa.

Kungani

Ukuze siqhube izifundo, sihlole imisebenzi, sikhumbuze ngokuphinda, sibhalisele ukusekelwa futhi sibonise isibali esiphelele. Igama odongeni lokubonga liboniswa kuphela ngemvume yakho, uma kungenjalo — «Ongaziwa».

Esingakwenzi

Asiyithengisi imininingwane, asiyisebenziseli ukukhangisa futhi asiyidluliseli kwabanye abantu, ngaphandle kwe-Telegram njengenkundla ye-bot nasezimeni lapho umthetho ukudinga. Imisebenzi yasekhaya ibonwa ngumbhali wesikole kuphela.

Izingane

Izifundo zifundelwa ingane ngumzali noma ngumuntu omdala. Asicele izingane imininingwane yomuntu siqu engaphezu kwaleyo edingekayo esifundweni.

Ukugcinwa

Imininingwane igcinwa kuseva yombhali uma nje usasebenzisa isikole, futhi isuswa uma ucela.

Amalungelo akho

Ungacela ukukhonjiswa, ukulungiswa noma ukususwa kwemininingwane yakho: bhalela i-bot uthi «susa imininingwane yami» (inkinobho ethi «✏️ Uthole iphutha?» noma umyalo /paysupport). Sizoyisusa imininingwane zingakapheli izinsuku ezingu-30.
